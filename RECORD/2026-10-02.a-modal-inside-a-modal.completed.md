# A modal inside a modal, and ESC to the chat

## The question

Jesús, in one message, on two things the page did:

> cuando el chat está oculto, al mostrarse, que tome el foco
> cuando el chat está mostrado, al pulsar ESC, si no tiene nada pendiente de
> cerrar, que tome el foco

> las modales relativas, como la de añadir un proveedor o un motor local,
> estas modales se mostrarían dentro de la modal padre, la modal hija saliendo
> del header de la modal padre
> la modal hija sin header, el overlay de la modal hija, sólo sobre el
> contenido de la modal padre

## What it was

- **The chat.** The logo called `setPane("chat")` and then focused
  `.composer input` in the same tick. That did nothing when the chat had been
  hidden, because the composer was not drawn yet. The column's own switch and
  ESC called `setPane` and never focused anything. ESC's last rung, with two
  columns, swapped content and chat whatever had the focus. With three
  columns it did nothing.
- **A modal opened from inside Settings** (the provider form, the engine
  form) was a second Settings: its own head and close button, centred on the
  window, and its own `::backdrop` over the whole page, Settings' head
  included. Nothing on screen said that the form belonged to Settings.

## What it is

### The chat takes the focus

- `showChatPane()` in `app.html` is now the only way the page shows the chat.
  It is `setPane("chat")` plus `focusChat()`, which focuses the composer one
  frame later, once a chat that was hidden has been drawn. The logo, the
  column's switch and ESC all call it.
- **ESC's last rung is now two steps.** With nothing left to cancel and the
  chat on screen, ESC focuses the composer. Only when the composer already
  has the focus does ESC swap the columns, in two-column mode. So from
  anywhere, ESC goes to the chat first. A second ESC goes to the content, and
  a third brings the chat back with the focus in it. With three columns, ESC
  only focuses.
- **The terminal and Monaco keep their ESC.** A keydown from inside
  `.terminal` or `#monaco-host`, or one that something already
  `preventDefault`ed, is left alone. A shell and vim are owed that key. Before
  this, ESC in the terminal could swap the columns.

### A child modal

`components/modal.html` checks, just before `showModal()`, whether its
`<dialog>` sits inside another open `dialog.modal`. If it does, the modal is
that one's **child**:

- **No head.** The title stays as the dialog's `aria-label`, and the forms
  have their own Cancel button.
- **A sheet hung from the parent's head.** It is centred over the parent's
  body at its `width`, with no top border and square top corners. It opens by
  sliding down out of the bottom edge of the head: a `translateY(-100%)` and a
  `clip-path: inset(100% …)` that run together, so the part still above that
  edge is never drawn. It leaves the same way.
- **The overlay is over the parent's body only.** It is the child's own
  `::backdrop`, sized to the parent's body. The parent's head stays lit, which
  is what shows whose form this is.

It is **still a `showModal()` dialog**, in the top layer. That choice is what
keeps it small. ESC goes to the topmost modal, so it closes the child and
leaves Settings open. The focus trap and the inert parent come from the
platform. A click on the overlay is a click on the dialog, so the
`@click.self` that already served as the backdrop dismisses the child. Only
the position changes. `fit()` measures the parent's box and the bottom of its
head, and writes them as `--top`, `--left`, `--w` and `--h` into the dialog's
style. The sheet and its `::backdrop` both read them. The measurement re-runs
on a `ResizeObserver` on the parent and on window resize. The component has
no unmount hook, so the first measurement after the dialog has left the
document disconnects both.

Every modal opened inside another becomes a child. That is what both such
modals today want. A modal that should open inside another *without* being
its child would need a prop. Nothing asks for one yet.

## Rejected

- **A `<div>` inside the parent instead of a second dialog.** It would sit in
  the parent's own box, so it would be clipped and placed with no measuring.
  But the focus trap, ESC and the inert body would all have to be written by
  hand, and the forms render inside a scrolled body. That body is not the
  parent's box, so the div would have to be moved out of its owner's
  template, and jq79 has no portal.
- **Positioning the child absolutely against the parent dialog.** That
  breaks the first time anything between the two is positioned, and the
  Settings sections are.
- **ESC that only focuses, dropping the swap.** In two columns the swap is
  the only key that reaches the content from the chat. Making it the second
  press instead of the first keeps it, and still makes the first press the
  one that was asked for.

## Found while building it

- **jq79 does not take a `$:` statement over several lines.** A ternary
  split across three lines broke the whole page with `Unexpected token '}'`.
  It now calls a function, `fitted(room, width)`, on one line.
- **A keyframe from `clip-path: inset(…)` to `none` does not interpolate.**
  It snaps halfway, and the sheet drew over the parent's head for the first
  half of its opening. Both ends are now an `inset()`. The negative sides
  leave room for the shadow.
- **`::backdrop` reads the dialog's custom properties** only because it now
  inherits from its originating element: Chrome 122, Firefox 120 and Safari
  17.4. Before those versions it would read none of them, and the overlay
  would fall back to the whole window. That is the old look, not a broken one.

## Checked

- The live smoke suite passed 29 of 29 against a fresh release binary.
  - The provider-form test now also asserts that the form has no
    `.modal-head`, that its top is the bottom of Settings' head (to within a
    pixel), and that ESC closes it while Settings stays open.
  - The old ESC test asserted that the first ESC after a modal swapped the
    columns. It now asserts the new order: chat with focus, then content,
    then chat with focus.
  - A new test covers ESC focusing the composer with three columns, and the
    column switch focusing it with two.
- A browser probe on a live server. The parent's body under the child's
  overlay went from rgb(237,237,237) to rgb(130,130,130). Outside the parent
  it stayed at 107, which is Settings' own backdrop, unchanged. A frame taken
  90 ms into the opening shows the sheet cut at the head's bottom edge.
- On a server with no provider, the composer is disabled and ESC cannot
  focus it. That is correct, and it is why the probe has to use a server
  that has one.

## Still open

- The engine form is a child through the same code and its test passes, but
  nobody has looked at it on screen.
- The static twin's suite (`site.spec.js`) was not run.
- While a child is open, the parent's head is inert, close button included.
  Closing Settings takes two steps, the child first. That is the platform's
  rule for a modal over a modal, and it was kept.
- Going from three columns to two redraws the chat, and the composer loses
  the focus it had. The next ESC gives it back.
