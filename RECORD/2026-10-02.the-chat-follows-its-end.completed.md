# The chat follows its end, and its head grows

## The questions

In one day, all of them about the chat column:

> qué tal si al componente Scrollable le añadimos la opción
> :autoscroll="bottom" […] por ejemplo en el chat, para que sea visible el
> contenido nuevo

> en el chat, cuando se está recibiendo una respuesta, el scroll parece
> bloqueado, creo que es debido a un autoscroll demasiado agresivo

> añade al header del chat: un botón de copiar; cuando está en modo layout
> responsive y se ven el editor y el chat, añade a la derecha del todo un botón
> para cambiar a modo 2 columnas […] este botón no cambia la preferencia de
> settings

> el button.columns que aparece en el chat y el que aparece en el editor,
> debería ser el mismo […] el button.swap debería ser el mismo

> la modal de contexto que se abre desde el footer del chat, no muestra la
> última respuesta

> en los snippet html, a la izquierda del botón de copiar, añade un botón para
> abrirlo en un iframe en una modal

## What it is

### Following the end

`Scrollable` takes `autoscroll="bottom"`, and `fill` for a box sized by a flex
column rather than by `max`. The transcript is inside one.

- **What it watches.** A mutation inside the scroller, which is how a
  streamed reply arrives. Also a resize of the scroller or of its children: a
  box that gets shorter, such as the gate pinned under it, would otherwise
  hide the end it was showing.
- **The first version locked the scroll.** It counted anything within 24px of
  the bottom as the end. A trackpad scrolls a few pixels an event, so a person
  scrolling up was still "at the end", and the next token pulled them back.
- **Now:**
  - The slack is 2px, which is what a zoomed page leaves.
  - A scroll up by wheel, key (↑, PageUp, Home) or touch leaves the end at
    once, before the scroll it started has landed.
  - The component's own scrolls never leave the end. By the time one lands,
    more may have arrived under it, and that is not somebody leaving.

### The chat's head

- **Copy.** It copies the conversation as text: the prompts quoted (`> `)
  and the answers as written. It is disabled with no messages.
- **The column switch** (`i-dock`, Chrome devtools' "dock to right"). It is
  last in the head, where the setting is *responsive* and the window has room
  for three columns.
  - **Not the setting.** `ui.twoColumns` lasts as long as the page is open.
    `General → Columns` is the setting, and this button is for now.
  - **Where it shows.** While it is on, it is in the content's head too, so
    two columns chosen from the chat can be undone from the column on screen
    half the time.
- **One control in both heads.** The swap and the column switch in the
  content's head sit in the same `.acts` as the chat's, with the same markup.
  The content's own `.swap` styles are gone. Computed styles were compared:
  colour, padding, radius and width are the same in both heads.

### The context modal shows the last answer

The modal shows what the newest turn sent, and the answer to it was written
after. That answer is now a last group, `answer`, dashed, marked *not sent
yet: the next turn carries it in history*. It follows the stream while it
grows.

### An HTML block opens as a page

On `html`/`htm`/`xhtml` snippets there is an eye left of copy. It opens a
`Preview` modal with the block in an `<iframe srcdoc>`, taken at the click so
that a reply still streaming does not reload it under a reader.

- **The frame has an origin of its own.** It is `sandbox="allow-scripts
  allow-modals"`, without `allow-same-origin`, so it cannot read this page,
  its token or its storage.
- **A CSP in a `<meta>` first in its head.** A later `<meta>` cannot loosen
  it. It sets `connect-src 'none'`, `form-action 'none'`, and scripts, styles,
  images and fonts from `https:` only. No `http:` means no `luu serve`.
- **The test is the image, not the `fetch`.** A `fetch` from an opaque origin
  fails on CORS alone, so it proves nothing. An `<img>` is not subject to
  CORS: the test asserts that one pointing at the server does not load, and a
  probe confirmed the same image does load in a frame with the same sandbox and
  no policy.

## Rejected

- **Autoscroll in the transcript alone.** It is a property of a box, and a
  log well wants it as much as the chat.
- **The column switch as the setting.** A setting is for always. This is
  pressed for the next ten minutes and then forgotten.
- **A preview that follows the stream.** A page reloading under somebody
  reading it is worse than a snapshot.

## Checked

- **Autoscroll:** a test grows rows at the end, scrolls up, grows more and
  asserts it was not moved, then scrolls back and asserts it follows. Another
  scrolls up 6px by wheel, less than the old slack, and asserts the position
  holds while more arrives.
- **The head:** a test narrows from the chat, switches to the content, undoes
  it from there, and asserts the setting in `localStorage` was not written. It
  also asserts the copy reaches the clipboard.
- **The context modal:** its test asserts the answer group, live and after a
  reload.
- **The preview:** `snippet.spec.js`, a server of its own with an HTML reply,
  asserts the page draws and its script runs, and that the image and the
  `fetch` to the server both fail.

## Still open

- A preview cannot reach public APIs either. That is the cost of
  `connect-src 'none'`.
